# Where to look for hackathons

The pages worth checking regularly for new Belgian hackathons. The list comes from the search that
produced the first set of listings on 6 October 2026. Add a page when it turns up events, and
remove one when it goes quiet.

## Recurring events with no recent edition

Worth a look once or twice a year in case they return.

- hey! Hackathon at FARI, Brussels: last seen 14 to 15 March 2025.
- Elia hackathon: fourth edition in March 2025.
- KISS Game Jam (January 2025) and Game Jam Franco-Belge in Namur (March 2025).
- Hack in the Woods, Louvain-la-Neuve: nothing after 2022.
- Startup Weekend: no Belgian city with confirmed dates.
- CASSINI, EUDIS, NASA Space Apps, Climathon, ETHGlobal, Junction: no Belgian site in 2025 or 2026.

## Known gaps

The first search found little or nothing in these places. That is more likely a limit of the search
than a sign that nothing happens there.

- Events announced only on LinkedIn, Meetup or in closed communities.
- UGent, ULB, VUB, UCLouvain and UAntwerpen faculties and student groups; 19, BeCode, MolenGeek.
- City digital teams, VRT, RTBF, De Lijn, SNCB, STIB, and Flemish banks, telecoms and utilities.
- The German-speaking community and the Province of Luxembourg.
- Legal tech, fintech, web3, construction, space and climate.
- MLH, Devfolio and Meetup were not searched one by one.
- KU Leuven pages could not be opened.
